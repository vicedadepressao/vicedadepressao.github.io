1. About the app

Vice Clips Publisher (“the app”) is an internal tool of Vice da Depressão. It edits gaming clips and publishes them, after human approval, to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook using their official APIs. Access is limited to members of our team.

2. Use of platforms

By using the app, team members must follow the terms and policies of each platform, including the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and the Meta Community Standards.

3. Content and credit

  • Every clip credits the original creator on screen and in the description.
  • We do not publish leaks, pre-release footage or story spoilers.
  • Creators can request the removal of any clip at leonida.clipes27@gmail.com; we remove it promptly.
  • Paid campaign content is clearly disclosed using each platform's branded-content tools.
