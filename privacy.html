Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Vice Clips Publisher (“the app”), operated by Vice da Depressão (“we”), handles information. The app is an internal tool used only by our team to edit gaming clips and publish them to the channel's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

1. Information we access

When our team connects one of our own accounts to the app through the platform's official login (OAuth), the app receives:

  • Access tokens issued by YouTube (Google), TikTok and Meta (Instagram/Facebook), which allow the app to publish videos to that account.
  • Basic account information needed to publish: account or channel ID, display name, username and avatar, and the posting options the platform offers (for example, privacy levels).
  • Information about videos we publish: video ID, link, publish status and public metrics such as view counts.

The app does not access other users' private data, messages, contacts or payment information.

2. How we use it

  • To upload and publish videos that a team member has reviewed and approved.
  • To show the publishing status and links of our own posts.
  • To read public metrics of our own posts to decide what content works.

We do not use this information for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and post records are stored in a private, access-controlled database and a private server used only by our team. Tokens are never published or shared. We delete tokens when an account is disconnected.

4. Retention and deletion

Post records are kept while the related video is online. You can ask us to delete any data at leonida.clipes27@gmail.com; we respond within 7 days.

5. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using features that interact with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

6. TikTok and Meta

Data received from TikTok is used only to publish and manage our own TikTok posts, in line with the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Developer Guidelines. Access granted to the app can be removed in the TikTok app under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions. Data received from Meta is handled according to the Meta Platform Terms, and access can be removed in Facebook Settings › Business integrations.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by adult members of our team.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: leonida.clipes27@gmail.com.

Política de Privacidade (resumo em português)

O Vice Clips Publisher é uma ferramenta interna do Vice da Depressão usada só pela nossa equipe para publicar vídeos nas nossas próprias contas. Ao conectar nossas contas pelo login oficial (OAuth) do YouTube, TikTok e Meta, o app recebe tokens de acesso, informações básicas da conta e dados dos vídeos que publicamos. Usamos isso apenas para publicar vídeos aprovados por uma pessoa da equipe e acompanhar as métricas públicas dos nossos posts. Não vendemos, não compartilhamos e não usamos esses dados para publicidade. Os dados ficam em banco e servidor privados. Para pedir exclusão de dados: leonida.clipes27@gmail.com (resposta em até 7 dias). O app usa os Serviços de API do YouTube: veja os Termos do YouTube e a Política de Privacidade do Google; o acesso pode ser revogado em myaccount.google.com/permissions.
