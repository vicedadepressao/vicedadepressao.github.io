The wildest GTA 6 moments, every day 🔥

Vice da Depressão is a gaming clips channel. We find the funniest and most surprising GTA 6 moments from streamers, edit them into vertical videos with captions, and always credit the original creator on screen and in the description.

Vice Clips Publisher

Vice Clips Publisher is our internal tool. It edits clips (vertical format, readable captions, creator credit) and, after a person on our team approves each video, publishes it to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook through their official APIs.

  • It is used only by the Vice da Depressão team to post to the channel's own accounts.
  • Every post is reviewed and approved by a human before publishing.
  • We never post on behalf of other people and never sell or share data.

▶ Uses YouTube API Services

Vice Clips Publisher uses the YouTube Data API to upload approved Shorts to our own YouTube channel, @vicedadepressao. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google security settings.
